Our Services

Complete Marine Engine & Systems Service

From diesel engine repair to routine maintenance plans, our certified technicians service every major system on board — engines, generators, electrical, plumbing, and steering.

Engine Service

Diesel Engine Repair

Whether you run twin diesels on a sportfisher or a single diesel inboard on a cruiser, our technicians diagnose and repair engine issues correctly the first time — minimizing downtime and unnecessary part replacement.

  • Complete engine diagnostics & troubleshooting
  • Overheating, starting, and performance issues
  • Injector, turbo, and cooling system repair
  • Oil analysis and scheduled engine tune-ups
Generator Service

Onboard Generator Repair & Installation

Reliable power on board matters — for air conditioning, appliances, and electronics. We service, repair, and install marine generators of all major brands and sizes.

  • Generator diagnostics and repair
  • Impeller, coolant, and exhaust service
  • Load testing and annual service plans
Electrical Systems

Marine Electrical Diagnostics & Repair

From nuisance electrical gremlins to full system upgrades, our technicians bring marine-grade electrical expertise to every panel, battery bank, and circuit on board.

  • Wiring, panel, and breaker repair
  • Battery bank design, install & charging systems
  • Shore power & inverter troubleshooting
  • Electronics & navigation system wiring
Marine Plumbing

Freshwater & Raw Water Plumbing

We repair and upgrade fresh water, raw water, bilge, and washdown plumbing systems so every system on board works the way it should.

  • Fresh water pump & water heater service
  • Raw water strainer & through-hull service
  • Bilge pump installation & repair
  • Hose, fitting, and seacock replacement
Sanitation Systems

Head & Sanitation System Repair

We tackle the jobs other shops avoid. Our technicians service and repair marine sanitation systems, holding tanks, and treatment units with minimal disruption on board.

  • Head & macerator pump repair
  • Holding tank service & odor elimination
  • Treatment system installation & repair
  • Y-valve & discharge system service
Hydraulic Steering

Hydraulic Steering Service & Repair

Steering failures are a safety concern. We inspect, bleed, and repair hydraulic steering systems to keep your helm responsive and dependable.

  • Hydraulic fluid bleeding & system flush
  • Helm pump & cylinder replacement
  • Steering cable & linkage inspection
  • Multi-station & autopilot integration checks
Preventative Maintenance

Scheduled Preventative Maintenance

The best repair is the one you never need. Our maintenance plans are built around your vessel's usage and Florida's marine environment to catch small issues before they become expensive ones.

  • Seasonal engine & generator service
  • Fluid, filter, and belt replacement schedules
  • Pre-trip & post-storage inspections
  • Detailed maintenance logs & reporting
