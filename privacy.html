Privacy Policy

What this website collects, and what we do with it.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Universal Marine Service is a family-owned marine engine and systems service business based at 17839 Courtside Landings Circle, Punta Gorda, FL 33955. This policy explains what information this website collects and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you submit the Request Service form, we receive the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address, mailing address, information about your vessel (name, manufacturer, model, year, length, hull number, and location), and your description of the problem or work requested. Only your name, phone, email, and description are required.

If you call or email us directly, we receive whatever information you choose to share.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your request, schedule and perform service, and contact you about your vessel. Form submissions are delivered to us by email. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies and third-party services

This website does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. Some pages include an embedded Google Map; Google may collect information such as your IP address and set its own cookies when a map loads, under Google's privacy policy. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) for security and troubleshooting.

How long we keep it

We keep service requests and customer correspondence for as long as needed to serve you and to maintain our business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information you have sent us by calling 239-246-1618 or emailing JosephPmuia@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new date.
